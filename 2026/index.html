About ICECE

The International Conference on Electrical and Computer Engineering (ICECE) is a pioneering international conference in the fields of electrical, electronic, communication, and computer engineering. It has been regularly organized biennially since 2001 by the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE) at Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET). ICECE brings together researchers, academics, industry professionals, and policymakers from around the world to exchange ideas and present their latest research findings and experiences. The Department of EEE, BUET is pleased to announce the 14th ICECE 2026, marking the 25-year milestone of this prestigious conference series. Prospective authors are invited to submit original technical papers for presentation at the conference. Accepted and presented papers will be submitted for inclusion into IEEE Xplore, subject to meeting IEEE Xplore's scope and quality requirements.

Call for Papers

ICECE 2026 invites original, unpublished research contributions in the fields of electrical, electronic, communication, and computer engineering. Submissions will undergo a double-blind peer review process, and accepted papers will be scheduled for presentation at the conference. Accepted and presented papers will be submitted for inclusion in IEEE Xplore, subject to meeting IEEE Xplore’s scope and quality requirements.

Instructions for Initial Paper Submission

Prospective authors are kindly invited to submit original technical papers to ICECE 2026 through the online paper submission system. All submitted papers must contain original, unpublished experimental and/or theoretical results. All submissions must be written in English only.

Submitted papers should satisfy the conference criteria and must not be under consideration for publication elsewhere.

Guide for Online Paper Submission
  1. Submit your paper through the Microsoft Conference Management Toolkit (CMT) link provided below.
  2. If you do not have a login ID, please register to create one.
  3. Name and affiliation of all authors must be entered in the CMT system. Inclusion or exclusion of an author is not allowed after notification of acceptance or during camera-ready submission.
  4. Open the paper submission website.
  5. Click Create New Submission, fill in the required information, and upload your PDF file.
  6. To add collaborators/co-authors, use the Enter email to add new author field. It is recommended that all co-authors create their CMT accounts before submission.
Camera-Ready Submission Guidelines

Authors are requested to check the formatting of the paper carefully prior to final submission. At least one author of each accepted paper must register before the registration deadline, and presentation at the allocated schedule during the conference is mandatory.

Inclusion or exclusion of an author is not allowed during camera-ready submission as per conference policy.

Step 1

Final Paper Preparation

  • Revise the manuscript according to reviewer comments.
  • Proofread carefully to ensure no further revision is required.
  • Use the standard IEEE conference template: IEEE templates
  • Maximum 6 pages including figures, tables, and references
  • Maximum file size: 10 MB
  • Paper size: US Letter or A4
  • At this stage, author names, affiliations, postal addresses, and email addresses must be included in the paper.
Step 2

IEEE PDF eXpress Compatibility

Convert your paper into an IEEE Xplore-compatible PDF using IEEE PDF eXpress .

First-time users:

  • Select New Users - Click Here
  • Enter the Conference ID (to be updated soon), email address, and password
  • Complete the remaining prompts

Previous users:

  • Log in with your username and password
  • Enter the Conference ID (to be updated soon)
  • Complete the remaining prompts

Once you login:

  • Upload the source file that contains your paper for conversion
  • Receive by email the IEEE Xplore-compatible PDF of your paper
  • Please along with IEEE PDF eXpress compliant pdf file preserve a copy of the compliance message from PDF eXpress which needs to be uploaded during your registration.

Please preserve a copy of the PDF eXpress compliance message, as it must be uploaded during registration.

Step 3

Camera-Ready PDF Upload in CMT

Log in to your Microsoft CMT Author system and upload the final camera-ready PDF by clicking Create Camera Ready Submission in the Actions column.

  • No password protection on the PDF file
  • All fonts must be embedded
  • All submitted papers must be written in English
Step 4

Electronic IEEE Copyright Submission

In the same Microsoft CMT Author system , click Submit the IEEE Copyright Form and complete the electronic copyright form (eCF).

A PDF copyright document will be generated automatically. A copy of this copyright form must be uploaded during final camera-ready submission as an additional document.

Step 5

Registration

Complete conference registration and pay the registration fee before the deadline mentioned on the registration page.

Step 6

Paper Presentation

Authors of registered papers are expected to present during December 10–12, 2026. The technical schedule and detailed presentation guidance will be published on the conference website.

Guide for Oral Presentation
  • Be present in the session room at least 15 minutes before the session starts.
  • Report to the session chair(s) before the session begins.
  • Upload your PowerPoint file to the PC provided in the session room.
  • The presentation time is 15 minutes including Q&A.
  • Remain present during the session, as the order of presentations may change due to unforeseen circumstances.
  • Answer questions from the session chairs and audience at the end.
  • A paper presented at the conference by a registered author will be submitted for inclusion into IEEE Xplore, subject to scope and quality requirements.
  • IEEE reserves the right to exclude a paper from distribution after the conference if the paper is not presented at the conference.
